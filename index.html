The practices in detail

1. Intentional conversation

Why it matters

Students talk more, and more honestly, when there is a regular, expected space to talk[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span). Conversation also has more substance when people don't take their time together for granted[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

At home

Try this week: pick one moment and ask the "best part, hardest part" question three times[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span). Listen first; don't fix[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).
2. Time well used: activities and systems

Why it matters

Young people need to try things to be ready for the life skills they will need[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span). Life needs to be explored and experienced[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span).

At home

Try this week: help your child pick one new activity and put a review date in the calendar[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span).
3. Intentional balance: plan the fun, and make space

Why it matters

Brains need to decompress or they burn out[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span). Fun must be part of the weekly rhythm, not just a rare reward[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span).

At home

Try this week: put something light on the busiest day[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span). If fun isn't landing, sit together quietly and hold space[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span).
4. Real responsibility

Why it matters

Students gain confidence and judgment when they make decisions that count and know they were heard[span_25](start_span)[span_25](end_span).

At home

Try this week: let your child plan one outing within a budget you set, then talk afterwards about what worked[span_27](start_span)[span_27](end_span).
5. Chores and giving back

Why it matters

Chores are small acts that exist for the good of the community[span_28](start_span)[span_28](end_span). Adults must model this by doing chores out in the open, showing everyone contributes equally[span_29](start_span)[span_29](end_span).

At home

Try this week: agree one daily chore per child and keep it for seven days[span_32](start_span)[span_32](end_span).
